# The Humble Village — Newcomers to the Program

This document serves as the final curated catalog of the four recent maternal and infant nutrition cases. It includes confirmed names, weights, ages, and medical details, along with photo selections and draft copy for the "Newcomers to the Program" section on the Stories page.

---

## 1. Baby Girl (RNF Yaxcal Cuz) — The Most Severe Case

This case represents the most severe age-to-weight deficit among the recent admissions and should lead the section to establish immediate urgency.

**Key Facts:**
- **Age at intake:** 1.5 months (nearly 7 weeks)
- **Weight at intake:** 4.4 lbs (born at 2.42 lbs)
- **Mother:** Single mother, scarce breastmilk, inverted nipples making breastfeeding difficult
- **Context:** At 7 weeks, a healthy baby should weigh approximately 9–10 lbs.

**Selected Photo:**
- `baby4-before.jpg` (or `RNF-YaxcalCuz-2.jpeg`) — Baby girl lying on a floral blanket, being held by caregiver's hands. The hands provide scale, emphasizing her extreme smallness.

**Draft Copy:**
> At nearly seven weeks old, she should weigh close to ten pounds. She weighs 4.4. Her single mother has scarce milk and struggles to breastfeed. This is what severe infant malnutrition looks like when it goes unaddressed—and it is happening every single day in Alta Verapaz. She is now with us. We are not letting go.

## 2. Eliza

Eliza's story highlights the compounding dangers of malnutrition and illness, particularly for adolescent mothers.

**Key Facts:**
- **Age at intake:** 22 days
- **Weight at intake:** 3.96 lbs
- **Medical history:** Hospitalized for 7 days with neonatal pneumonia
- **Mother:** Single mother, age 14, difficulty breastfeeding due to nipple size

**Selected Photo:**
- `eliza-mother.jpg` — Eliza's 14-year-old mother holding the bundled baby and a can of NAN formula. This photo centers the adolescent mother's experience.

**Draft Copy:**
> Baby Eliza arrived at 22 days old weighing just 3.96 pounds. She had already spent seven days in the hospital fighting neonatal pneumonia. Her mother is 14 years old, single, and struggling to breastfeed. Eliza is now in our Maternal Nutrition Program. We are fighting for her.

## 3. Baby Boy (RNM Chub Choc)

This case underscores the extreme vulnerability of families dealing with severe maternal disability and the systemic failure of discharging such fragile infants.

**Key Facts:**
- **Age at intake:** 20 days
- **Weight at intake:** 4.18 lbs
- **Mother:** Single mother, deaf, mute, and suffering from epileptic seizures

**Selected Photo:**
- `baby3-before.jpg` (or `RNM-ChubChoc.jpeg`) — Baby boy lying on a patterned blanket.

**Draft Copy:**
> He arrived at 20 days old weighing 4.18 pounds. His single mother is deaf, mute, and lives with epilepsy. The hospital sent them home. We didn't. He is now in our Maternal Nutrition Program, receiving formula and care. We are watching over both of them.

---

## 4. Liam (Norma's Baby) — Proof of Progress

Liam's story closes the section on a note of hope, providing concrete evidence that the intervention works quickly and effectively.

**Key Facts:**
- **Age at intake:** 7 days
- **Weight at intake:** 3.52 lbs
- **Mother:** Norma (age 15), has jaundice, inverted nipples, and insufficient milk
- **Progress:** Gained from 3.96 lbs to 5.5 lbs in one week at the center
- **Treatment:** NAN formula + maternal Incaparina

**Selected Photo:**
- `Liam.jpeg` — The new, clear photo of Liam lying on the yellow/green elephant blanket, showing his jaundice.

**Draft Copy:**
> Liam arrived at just 7 days old weighing 3.52 pounds. His 15-year-old mother, Norma, was suffering from jaundice and unable to produce enough milk. We immediately provided formula for Liam and maternal Incaparina for Norma. Within one week of entering the program, Liam gained over a pound and a half. This is the work in action.
